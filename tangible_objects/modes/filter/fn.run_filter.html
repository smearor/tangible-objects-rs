Filter-proxy mode (FILTER_PROXY_VIRTUAL.md): grabs the real device and re-emits its event stream on a uinput clone, minus the slots a matched tangible occupies.

Only exact matches suppress - partial candidates carry a widened tolerance that would eat finger touches resembling a profile. A matched slot stays suppressed across transient match loss until its tracking contact ends or the filter’s hysteresis expires (Figur7/8-style flappy pins must not leak to the OS). SetFilterMode control commands switch the suppression policy live (off/immediate/gated/guarded).
