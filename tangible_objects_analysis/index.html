Crate tangible_objects_analysis 

Source
Expand description

Offline analysis of training recordings (TRAINING.md phase 2).

Recording loads a JSONL artifact written by --record; the reports - UtilizationReport, ToleranceReport, FlappyReport and CollisionReport - turn the recorded candidates into tolerance decisions. All evaluation happens offline, never in the hot detection loop.
