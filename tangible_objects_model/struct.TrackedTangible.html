Expand description

A tangible figure tracked across frames with a smoothed pose.

Produced by the engine’s Tracker; position and rotation are exponentially smoothed over consecutive frames. Serializes to the interface-facing flat shape {id, pattern_id, pattern_name, center_x, center_y, rotation_deg} - millimeters and degrees, without the internal frames_since_match hysteresis counter.

Fields§

§id: TangibleId

Stable ID of this tangible, assigned by the Tracker on creation. Distinguishes tangibles matching the same pattern.

§pattern_id: PatternId

ID of the matched pattern.

§pattern_name: PatternName

Name of the matched figure profile.

§center: Vec2<Millimeters>

Smoothed centroid position.

§rotation_rad: RotationRad

Smoothed orientation in radians.

§pending: bool

Whether this tangible was spawned by a partial match and has not yet confirmed (SPAWN_AS_PARTIAL.md). Pending tangibles reserve their slots but emit no events until the spawn-stability window or an exact match confirms them.

§pending_frames: FrameCount

Consecutive match frames the pending spawn has held so far; internal bookkeeping, not exposed on interfaces.

§spawn_stability: FrameCount

Confirmation threshold carried from the pattern; internal bookkeeping, not exposed on interfaces.

§frames_since_match: FrameCount

Number of consecutive frames without a match for this tangible. Used for expiry hysteresis; internal bookkeeping, not exposed on interfaces.
